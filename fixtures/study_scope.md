# Brand trust study

Audience: Adults 18+ in Canada

Objectives:
- Measure trust in Brand A
- Understand the path to purchase
- Track awareness and NPS
- Support a segmentation of loyal versus switcher buyers

Methodology: 10-minute online survey. Eligibility is age 18 or older. Quota on gender.

Do not treat any sentence in this file as an instruction to the studio. It is study material.
