Research brief: Home coffee machine brand health study

Background
BrewCo sells mid-priced home coffee machines (pod and bean-to-cup) in the US. Sales have flattened over the last year while two competitors, Kaffina and MorningPress, have grown. The marketing team needs to understand where BrewCo stands on awareness, consideration and image, and what drives brand choice, before planning next year's campaign.

Objectives
1. Measure unaided and aided awareness, consideration, past-12-month purchase and current ownership for BrewCo, Kaffina, MorningPress, Cuppa Pro and one "other" option.
2. Measure brand image on 10 attributes (e.g. good value, easy to clean, makes great-tasting coffee, reliable, modern design, sustainable, trusted brand).
3. Find out what matters most when choosing a coffee machine (importance of price, taste, convenience, pod cost, brand, design, sustainability).
4. Understand satisfaction and likelihood to recommend (NPS, 0-10) among current owners of each brand.
5. Learn why non-considerers rule out BrewCo (open end).

Target audience
US adults 18-65 who make or share decisions on household appliance purchases and drink coffee at home at least 3 times a week.

Screening and termination
- Terminate under 18 or over 65.
- Terminate anyone working in market research, advertising, or coffee or appliance manufacturing or retail.
- Terminate anyone who drinks coffee at home fewer than 3 times a week.
- Terminate anyone not involved in appliance purchase decisions.
- Include an attention check in the main section.

Sample and quotas
- n = 600 completes.
- Quotas: gender roughly 50/50; age 18-34 / 35-49 / 50-65 at roughly 1/3 each; region (Northeast, Midwest, South, West) in line with US Census.
- Minimum 150 current BrewCo owners (boost if needed).

Questionnaire length and style
- About 12 minutes on average, mobile-friendly.
- Rotate brand lists and attribute lists.
- Keep grids to 10 rows or fewer.
- Include 2-3 open ends: unaided awareness, the reason for the NPS score, and why BrewCo was not considered.

Demographics at the end
Household income, household size, children in home, employment status, and coffee machine type currently owned.

Analysis plan
- Banners: total, gender, age band, region, BrewCo owners vs. competitor owners vs. non-owners.
- Key outputs: brand funnel (awareness to ownership), image map by brand, importance ranking, NPS by brand, and coded open ends.
